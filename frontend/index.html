RESEARCH MATERIALS

Choices made
for this study.

Each resource serves a defined role in the experiment. The pilot holds its generator constant to compare prompts, while screening signals are treated as exploratory.

01 / DATASET

XL-Sum Hindi

A Hindi news summarization test set gives the baseline aligned source and reference summaries for a paired comparison.

Hasan et al. · Findings ACL 2021 ↗
02 / BASELINE

mT5 multilingual XLSum

A multilingual summarizer fine-tuned for XL-Sum provides a reproducible Hindi baseline. It is not a new model developed in this project.

Checkpoint model card ↗
03 / CODE-MIXED DATA

Synthetic p100 + MUTANT

Paired synthetic inputs isolate a controlled code-mixing condition; MUTANT adds naturally occurring Hinglish. Synthetic text is not a substitute for authentic code-mixing.

Gupta et al. · Findings EACL 2023 ↗
04 / PROMPT PILOT

Qwen3-4B

One generator is held fixed while standard and factuality-aware instructions are compared on matched sources. The pilot does not compare LLM families.

Checkpoint model card ↗
05 / AUTOMATED SCREEN

mDeBERTa NLI

Multilingual entailment scores provide a source-summary screening signal. Near-ceiling scores in this pilot limit their ability to distinguish outputs.

Model card and training data ↗
METHOD INSPIRATION

FactCC

FactCC informs the source-grounded factuality-checking idea. Its English trained model is not used or claimed to validate Hindi results here.

Kryściński et al. · EMNLP 2020 ↗

Dataset and checkpoint authors retain credit for the underlying resources. Project-specific data preparation, prompt comparison, audits, and reporting are documented separately.
